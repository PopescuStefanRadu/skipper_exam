# Learning and review

## Behaviour

- **Learning mode:** go through questions by topic or in order, with immediate feedback and the [explanation](explanations.md) after each answer.
- **Practice my mistakes:** a session built from questions you've answered wrong, prioritising recent and repeated mistakes.
- **Coverage and weak topics:** how much of each question list you've seen, and where you fail most.
- All answers count toward your history, whatever the mode.

## E2E scenarios

Main paths:
- Learning mode: answer a question and get immediate feedback and the explanation.
- "Practice my mistakes" contains exactly the questions answered wrong before.

Less common paths:
- With no mistakes yet, "practice my mistakes" says there's nothing to review instead of starting an empty session.
